Touchord Cheat Sheet

Touchord cheat sheet

Green keys play chords. White buttons change things: tap for a quick action, or hold and slide on the touch bar to choose. On the bar, the far end always means more.

Tap

A quick action. B0 next mode, B1 octave down, B2 octave up. B3 to B5 do nothing on a tap.

Hold + slide

Hold a white button, then touch the bar and slide. The screen shows the choices as a strip and plays the first chord so you hear it.

Far = more

Away from you on the bar is higher, faster, brighter and harder, in every mode and every menu.

The six white buttons

Each strip below reads like the bar: far end at the top, near end at the bottom. The highlighted choice is the one you start with.

Combinations

B0 hold + tap 1-7

Recall a song memory

Switches the whole setup. Your MIDI and Bluetooth settings stay.

B0 hold + 1-7 hold 2 s

Save a song memory

Wait for "Saved to". Your setup also saves itself a few seconds after each change.

B1 + B2 hold

Help screen

Lists what every button and the bar do in the current mode.

B0 + B2 + B4 hold 3 s

Firmware update

The screen counts down. Let go to cancel.

Worth knowing: Settings › MIDI › Quantize lines presses up with the beat (Off, 1/16, 1/8, 1/4, 1 Bar). It is Off out of the box; when on, a small Q shows at the bottom left of the screen.
